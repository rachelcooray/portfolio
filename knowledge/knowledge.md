## 02 How I work

**Analytics that works in the field, not just in a model**
I make sure analytics works in the field, not just in a model. For each use case I design how we check whether it made a difference, using pilot and control groups where possible and before-and-after comparisons where not. I start with operational measures such as billing activity, outlet reach and how closely recommendations are followed, then look at margin. Where there is no control group, I describe results as an association, not proven impact. I join governance calls with area, regional and sales managers and bring what I learn back into the use case. I help prepare answers to finance and leadership challenges. I keep decision logs, process maps and problem statements so the reasoning is still there months later, and I reconcile numbers across dashboards and own the corrections.

## 03 Research

**PCOS Care: A Machine Learning-Based Web Application for Early Risk Prediction of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome**
IEEE ICAHS 2025, Tunisia. An early screening web app with two levels of prediction: one from everyday signs that anyone can answer, and one that adds hormone test results. It shows which factors drove each prediction. It is a screening aid, not a diagnostic tool. Method: Chi-Square and ANOVA feature selection, SMOTE for class balance, and an enhanced Random Forest. Third place at the University of Westminster final year project showcase, 2025. Extended paper selected for the MSIT Journal special edition, under review.
