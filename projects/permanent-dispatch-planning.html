Permanent Dispatch Planning

How can dispatch planning be improved while reducing transport costs?

Supports permanent dispatch planning through a more structured, data-driven approach to distribution decisions.

Role
Business requirements, delivery coordination, process understanding and validation.
Technologies
Python, PySpark, SQL, Databricks, Delta Lake, Power BI, Excel
Timeline
Oct 2025 to present
Domain
FMCG distribution, OCTAVE

Confidentiality: shown without client data, internal architecture, commercial figures or operational detail. Diagrams are illustrative.

01

The problem

Improving distribution planning while minimising lorry costs.

02

Context and constraints

Dispatch decisions balance distribution requirements against operational constraints and transport cost.

03

My approach

A more structured, data-driven approach to dispatch and distribution decisions.

04

Solution and architecture

A dispatch planning system designed to improve distribution planning and minimise lorry costs.

05

Implementation

  • Business requirements.
  • Delivery coordination.
  • Process understanding and validation.

06

Evaluation and validation

  • Pilot and control groups where possible, before and after comparisons where not.
  • Operational measures first, such as billing activity, outlet reach and how closely recommendations are followed, then commercial outcomes.
  • Where there is no control group, results are described as an association, not proven impact.
