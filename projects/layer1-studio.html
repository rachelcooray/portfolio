Software engineering

Layer1.Studio digital products

How does a rough brief become a product a small business can run?

Websites, mobile applications and digital experiences for clients across different industries.

Role
Requirements, design, development, deployment and maintenance, with interns on real client projects.
Technologies
Flutter, React, Node.js
Timeline
Dec 2025 to present
Domain
Small business products

01

The problem

Small businesses arrive with an informal brief, a few spreadsheets and a process held together by messages and paper.

02

Context and constraints

The product has to be one the owner can run without us: scoped tightly, shipped quickly and maintained after launch.

03

My approach

  • Shape the brief into a short architecture: a responsive web platform and, where needed, a companion mobile app.
  • Build the operational pieces first, such as sign-ups, bookings, payments and reminders.
  • A lightweight serverless stack and optimistic UI updates, so products stay responsive on ordinary mobile networks.

04

Solution and architecture

Products in use across fitness, food, e-commerce, marketing, arts and publishing, workplace culture and sports. No client is named here without permission.

05

Implementation

  • Client requirements and scoping.
  • Interface design, architecture and backend integration.
  • Development, deployment and maintenance.
  • Mentoring interns on real client projects.

06

Results and limitations

A repeatable delivery model: brief, scope, build, launch, maintain.

07

Lessons and next steps

Building for small business operators means designing for operational friction first.
