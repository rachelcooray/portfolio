Ice Cream Distributor Margins

How can distributor-level data support a clearer understanding of commercial performance?

Brings together distributor sales, targets, product information and performance measures to support margin analysis and commercial decision-making.

Role
Analytics delivery, data validation, business requirements and finance-facing performance reporting.
Technologies
Python, PySpark, SQL, Databricks, Delta Lake, Power BI, Excel
Timeline
Oct 2025 to present
Domain
FMCG distribution, OCTAVE

Confidentiality: shown without client data, internal architecture, commercial figures or operational detail. Diagrams are illustrative.

01

The problem

Understanding distributor performance and margin outcomes clearly enough to design commercial incentives around them.

02

Context and constraints

A distributor network where performance, targets and incentives all affect margin.

03

My approach

  • Brings together distributor sales, targets, product information and performance measures.
  • Analytics and commercial incentive mechanisms built on that combined view.

04

Solution and architecture

Analytics and incentive mechanisms that make distributor performance and margin outcomes visible.

05

Implementation

  • Analytics delivery and business requirements.
  • Data validation.
  • Finance-facing performance reporting.

06

Evaluation and validation

  • Pilot and control groups where possible, before and after comparisons where not.
  • Operational measures first, such as billing activity, outlet reach and how closely recommendations are followed, then commercial outcomes.
  • Where there is no control group, results are described as an association, not proven impact.

07

Lessons and next steps

A model is only useful if the incentive it recommends is one a distributor team can actually run.
