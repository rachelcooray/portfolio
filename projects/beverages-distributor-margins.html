Beverages Distributor Margins

How can commercial performance be measured and understood across a distributor network?

Applies analytics to distributor-level performance and commercial margin management.

Role
Use-case coordination, stakeholder engagement, analytical validation and reporting.
Technologies
Python, PySpark, SQL, Databricks, Delta Lake, Power BI, Excel
Timeline
Oct 2025 to present
Domain
FMCG distribution, OCTAVE

Confidentiality: shown without client data, internal architecture, commercial figures or operational detail. Diagrams are illustrative.

01

The problem

Measuring commercial performance consistently across a distributor network.

02

Context and constraints

Distributor-level performance has to be understood before margins can be managed.

03

My approach

Distributor-level analytics for performance measurement and margin management.

04

Solution and architecture

Distributor-level analytics that support commercial performance and margin management.

05

Implementation

  • Use-case coordination.
  • Stakeholder engagement.
  • Analytical validation and reporting.

06

Evaluation and validation

  • Pilot and control groups where possible, before and after comparisons where not.
  • Operational measures first, such as billing activity, outlet reach and how closely recommendations are followed, then commercial outcomes.
  • Where there is no control group, results are described as an association, not proven impact.
