PCOS Care · Research case study

How the study was built, step by step.

Only the published method is shown. Results figures are not repeated here: read them in the paper.

01Research question

Can machine learning help people identify potential PCOS risk earlier, through an accessible web application?

02Motivation and background

PCOS is a complex condition with varied symptoms. Many risk models produce opaque scores, and models that need full hormone panels do not help where those tests are not available.

03Dataset

An anonymised public benchmark dataset of patient records from hospitals in Kerala, India.

04Feature selection

Chi-Square tests for categorical indicators and ANOVA F-tests for numerical ones, keeping signals a clinician would recognise.

05Class balancing

SMOTE, to address the imbalance between PCOS and non-PCOS cases.

06Model development

An enhanced Random Forest.

07Evaluation

Stratified cross-validation favouring recall, since a missed case costs more than a false alarm, and a structured usability study with real users.

08Web application

Two pathways: an initial screen from everyday signs and symptoms, and an enhanced screen that adds hormone test results. Each result shows the factors behind it. Built in Python, Flask and Streamlit, and deployed.

09Limitations and future work

A screening-support tool, not a diagnostic system or a replacement for medical advice. Trained on one public benchmark dataset.
