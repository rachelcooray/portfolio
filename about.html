02 About · Background and philosophy

Curiosity. Impact. Always learning.

I have always been interested in understanding how things work: not just technically, but how they connect to people, organisations and the world around us.

My journeyMy work

Biography

A curious mind, a multidisciplinary approach.

My journey began with an interest in computing and problem-solving. Studying Computer Science gave me a foundation in software engineering, data science and analytical thinking. Working on real business problems then introduced me to commercial decision-making, project delivery, stakeholder management, research, communication and leadership.

I found that the most interesting problems rarely sit within one discipline.

Today I work in Data and AI at OCTAVE, part of John Keells Holdings, on technology and analytics initiatives in the FMCG sector. Alongside this, I co-founded Layer1.Studio, where I work on digital products and software for businesses.

My interests extend into academic research, responsible technology, entrepreneurship and community. I don't see these as unrelated pursuits. They are different ways of understanding problems, developing ideas and creating value.

Professional philosophy

How I approach problems.

  1. 01

    Understand before building

    Good solutions begin with understanding the problem, the people affected and the environment the solution must work in.

  2. 02

    Think across disciplines

    Technology, business, design, research and human behaviour are connected. I enjoy bringing these perspectives together.

  3. 03

    Build with purpose

    I value practical outcomes, not technology for its own sake. A solution should be useful, understandable and sustainable.

  4. 04

    Keep learning

    I want to keep a broad foundation while developing genuine depth in areas that can make a difference.

Personal values

What I hold myself to.

Curiosity

Asking how things work, and why, before deciding what to build.

Integrity

Reporting what the evidence supports, including association rather than impact.

Adaptability

Moving between data, delivery, research and people as the problem needs.

Impact

Judging work by whether people can use it, not by how impressive it looks.

Career ambition

Broad foundations. Deeper expertise.

I am building towards a career that combines technical understanding, research, organisational problem-solving and leadership. I'm interested in how intelligent systems move beyond experimentation into real-world use, and how to make that transition effective, responsible and human-centred.

  • Applied artificial intelligence and machine learning
  • Human-centred intelligent systems
  • Explainability and trust in AI
  • Responsible AI and governance
  • Enterprise AI adoption and decision intelligence
  • Technology and organisational performance

An evolving area of interest, not a claim that I already specialise in all of these fields.
